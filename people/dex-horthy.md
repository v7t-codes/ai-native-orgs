# Dex Horthy

Founder of HumanLayer, now a member of technical staff at Factory. Wrote 12-factor agents and argues for human review of small vertical slices over lights-out software factories.

**Where to follow:**
- X: [@dexhorthy](https://x.com/dexhorthy)
- GitHub: [humanlayer](https://github.com/humanlayer)

## Best pieces

- 🟢 **[12-Factor Agents](https://github.com/humanlayer/12-factor-agents)** — Dex Horthy, 2025-03
  Twelve principles for LLM software good enough for production customers, written after the 12-factor app. Among them: own your prompts, own your context window, small focused agents, and make the agent a stateless reducer over its event history.
  Tags: `agent-design`, `context-engineering`, `foundational`

- **[Why Software Factories Fail: Turning the lights back on (part 2)](https://x.com/dexhorthy/status/2081058573556306030)** — Dex Horthy, 2026-07
  A counterweight to lights-out factories. About 40% of tasks one-shot or need light feedback; larger ones go through four planning phases so humans can review and re-steer 100-200 line vertical slices, and the post argues for a safe 2-3x speedup. Its line: "You don't have too many PRs. You have too many bad PRs."
  Tags: `software-factory`, `planning`, `review`

## Why Dex matters here

12-factor agents (27,000+ GitHub stars) is a widely used design checklist, and the software-factory posts are the clearest case for keeping humans on the plan. See [SDLC & coding loops](../topics/sdlc-coding.md).
