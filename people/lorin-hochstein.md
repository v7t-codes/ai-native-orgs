# Lorin Hochstein

Resilience engineer (Airbnb, ex-Netflix). Writes one of the few critical voices in the AI-SRE space.

**Where to follow:**
- Blog: [surfingcomplexity.blog](https://surfingcomplexity.blog)

## Best pieces

- 🟢 **[Lots of AI SRE, no AI Incident Management](https://surfingcomplexity.blog/2026/02/14/lots-of-ai-sre-no-ai-incident-management/)** — Lorin Hochstein, 2026-02
  The missing critical voice. Reviews PagerDuty SRE Agents, Datadog Bits AI SRE, incident.io AI SRE, Microsoft Azure SRE Agent, Rootly AI SRE, Cleric, Resolve.ai, Anyshift.io, RunWhen — and argues these tools do automated diagnostics + maybe remediation, but **the core of SRE work is coordination**, not detection. An effective AI incident manager would need to model responder mental states and proactively re-establish common ground when responders are stuck. Nothing on the market does that.
  Tags: `ai-sre`, `incident-management`, `practitioner-bylined`, `must-read`

- **[Resilience category index](https://surfingcomplexity.blog/category/resilience/)** — Lorin Hochstein
  Multiple posts on resilience engineering — pre-AI but the principles directly transfer to agent failure modes.
  Tags: `resilience`, `index`

## Why he matters here

If everything else you read in this repo is bullish on AI, read Lorin to recalibrate. He's not anti-AI — he's anti-handwaving. His writing is on what AI in SRE *won't* do, which is the more useful frame than what it will.
