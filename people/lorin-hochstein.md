# Lorin Hochstein

Resilience engineer (ex-Netflix, Amazon Prime Video). Writes one of the few critical voices in the AI-SRE space.

**Where to follow:**
- Blog: [surfingcomplexity.blog](https://surfingcomplexity.blog)

## Best pieces

- 🟢 **[Lots of AI SRE, no AI Incident Management](https://surfingcomplexity.blog/...)** — the missing critical voice. Argues current "AI for SRE" tools mostly help with *detection*, not with the messy human-coordinated work of incident response.

## Why he matters here

If everything else you read in this repo is bullish on AI, read Lorin to recalibrate. He's not anti-AI — he's anti-handwaving. Most of his recent writing is on what AI in SRE *won't* do, which is the more useful frame than what it will.
