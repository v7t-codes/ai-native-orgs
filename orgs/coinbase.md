# Coinbase

> One of the highest-profile examples of "an agent for every employee" as an org strategy, not just engineering.

**The headline:** Coinbase rolled out personal AI agents to every employee, with internal tooling and data layer to support them. The eng side is interesting because it's a non-AI-native company doing a wholesale agent rollout, which is closer to what most enterprise orgs need to figure out.
