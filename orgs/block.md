# Block

> One of the cleanest org-wide rollout case studies — published an actual maturity ladder and adoption numbers.

**The headline:** **95% adoption** across engineering. Largest population at **Stage 5** (single agent, mostly outside the IDE), second-largest at **Stage 6** (3–5 agents in parallel). They publish a Stage-1 through Stage-6 maturity ladder with what each stage required to reach.
