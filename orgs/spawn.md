# Spawn

> Spawn is a ten-person games company run by permanent agents that each own a part of the business, from the engine to finance. Its founder's X article is the most extreme public account of an agent-run company, with numbers and named failures.

**The headline:** Token spend is about 30x payroll ($3.1M in one month), and 1,055 of 123,548 changes were reverted over a year, about 0.9%. All figures are the founder's own.

## Practitioner sources

- **[How I Built the Most AI-Pilled Company in the World](https://x.com/jsnnsa/status/2115623382721126826)** — Jacob (@jsnnsa), 2026-10
  Spawn dropped pull requests: agents work directly on the code, live product and database, helper agents that did not write a change try to break it before it lands, and the full test suite runs against the latest code every 15 minutes. Two years of recorded calls and chats were turned into principles for each person's taste, and AI judges applying them decided 7,296 questions, sent 2,213 back and held 1,863 for a human in eight days. The failures are the useful part: a model asked to secure the product disabled every feature, and one run spent $100,000 over two days for 1-3% gains. Borderline: founder self-report, partly promotional.
